**TL;DR:** Quick-lookup pages for once you already know the concepts and just need to check a detail.

Every other chapter on this site teaches a concept once, in depth, with reasoning and examples. This one is different on purpose: dense, scannable tables, with as little repeated explanation as possible. If a table entry doesn't make sense on its own, that's the signal to follow its link back to the chapter that actually taught it — this chapter exists to help you recall something you've already learned, not to teach it for the first time.
