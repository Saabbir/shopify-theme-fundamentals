**TL;DR:** Serving correctly-sized images, video and 3D media, and icons — without hurting performance.

You've used `image_url` since [Common Filters by Category](/liquid-basics/common-filters-by-category/), and read an image object's own properties since [Other Useful Objects](/global-objects/other-useful-objects/). This chapter builds a complete, production-quality `<img>` on top of that foundation, then covers the two other common media types a theme handles: video (including 3D/AR), and icons.
